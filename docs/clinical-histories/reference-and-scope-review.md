# Clinical capture and reference review

Implementation phase: 3. Reviewed by Codex, the user's designated synthetic domain reviewer. This records a technical and synthetic plausibility review, not licensed clinical approval. No clinical prevalence, dose-response model, length of stay or laboratory reference interval is represented as an externally validated clinical benchmark.

The accepted master snapshot supplies the realistic public-aggregate-weighted names, birth dates, fictional addresses, coverage, workforce, source identities and opening conditions. Faker 40.15.0 was evaluated. Its [official reproducibility documentation](https://faker.readthedocs.io/en/stable/index.html) explains version-sensitive seeded draws and instance seeding. Replacing the already accepted weighted identities or independently fabricating clinical sentences would weaken this dataset's longitudinal consistency. The clinical generator therefore uses the existing stateless seeded engine and structured, varied fact rendering; Faker is not a runtime dependency.

## Medication rule review

These four local products are selected examples of oral care evidence, not a complete formulary or a prescribing recommendation. The generated order is checked against the exact configured product, formulation, amount, route, interval, maximum, duration and recorded allergen family. The model captures no pediatric, pregnancy, infusion, anuric renal failure, hepatic failure, interacting combination or complex titration protocol. Such unrecorded characteristics are unknown; this validator does not certify individual clinical suitability. Broad allergen families remain conservative synthetic matching rules and do not claim verified immunological cross-reactivity.

## Clinical pathway and semantics review

Heart failure, COPD, pneumonia, general medicine/diabetes assessment, elective calculous cholecystitis and right-knee osteoarthritis have explicit diagnosis, observation, repeated inpatient analyte, selected procedure and treatment evidence. General medicine includes glucose assessment and education; definitive glucose management and the remainder of each care regimen are outside this selected capture. Oral doses and procedures are provisional demand; resource integration can change timing and withhold services. The generated observations represent selected points, not complete bedside telemetry. A patient-specific latent illness state drives related acute values and recovery; CKD changes the creatinine context. Normal intervals are declared fictional adult laboratory context. No independent normal draw is used for every result. Two additional ambulatory symptom pathways use R079 and R1010 without asserting a definitive cause or an ACS rule-out. They distinguish episodic complaints from known chronic follow-up. Troponin I uses a fictional assay-specific interval; it does not establish diagnostic exclusion.

Local education/assessment services and actual PCS surgical procedures are distinguished. Laparoscopic gallbladder resection uses 0FT44ZZ with K8010; right knee cemented synthetic replacement uses 0SRC0J9 with M1711. ICD releases are taken from the accepted dated code catalog. Clinical diagnosis assertions use their assertion date; final institutional claim recoding at discharge is a later billing-phase responsibility. A resolving assertion retains the original diagnosis code rather than recoding the disease on each status update.

[FHIR R4 Observation](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/observation.html) and [MedicationAdministration](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/medicationadministration.html) were used to distinguish observation, order, performance, administration and documentation semantics. Output is the frozen canonical schema, not a conformant FHIR export.

Death is observed from a terminal encounter disposition. Registration retains its independently accepted, unknown death field until a later source update; null never means alive. Terminal episodes occur in the final days of December, and no later care is generated. This source-latency scenario is explicit; a global death oracle uses both clinical and registration sources. Every pending follow-up and open admission is censored at the local business cutoff. Future intended therapy/appointment dates are plans, not observed services.

The synthetic plausibility reviewer checks all selected longitudinal summaries and notes, alongside the independent full-cohort checks and deliberate invalid-data tests. Any unsupported clinical inference remains a limitation rather than being marked clinically validated. Actual capacity, stock and financial reconciliation cannot pass until their later implementation phases.

The inpatient surgical cohort deliberately represents longer-stay selected inpatient care. Its frozen LOS calibration is not a day-case cholecystectomy or arthroplasty benchmark. Complete anesthetic, perioperative medication and clinical contraindication detail is outside this selected extract, and clinical effectiveness/suitability is not marked validated.
