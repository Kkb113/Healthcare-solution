# Longitudinal clinical generation design

Implementation phase: 3. Frozen business schema: baseline 1.0.0, unchanged. All new artifacts use descriptive names.

## Cohort and immutable source foundation

Preserve every one of the 78,922 accepted master rows, including all identity versions and 13,000 opening condition/allergy assertions. Pin the accepted canonical business hash and independently rerun master rules before consuming it. New source code does not change this historical snapshot's generation identifiers. Rebuild it from the exact accepted Git revision when needed, without resetting the current checkout or weakening its acceptance hash.

Generate 58,000 distinct encounter contexts: 12,000 intersecting inpatient admissions (57 occupied carry-ins), 27,000 ED visits including 6,000 admitted transitions, 17,000 outpatient visits, and 2,000 completed December 2022 opening-only contexts. There are 20,000 active stable patients. Eight thousand have one in-window visit; 12,000 are eligible for repeated activity, with the measured repeat share validated against the declared target. Within 10,680 inpatient people, 1,320 have a deliberately linked unplanned medical return 8–25 days after discharge. Returns pass through their own ED encounter and a new episode. The 1,320 linked returns are not automatically the readmission-rate numerator: death, transfers and incomplete 30-day follow-up are excluded independently from eligible home discharges. Continuous ED-to-inpatient care shares an episode; unrelated follow-up does not.

First presentation times are reproducible stochastic clinical demand. Elective admissions and clinic activity have daytime/weekday structure; the selected operations occur three hours after planned admission and use the relevant General Surgery or Orthopedics attending/author specialty; all timestamps are UTC derived from the pinned Chicago calendar. Patient-level scheduling avoids overlapping encounters. Full facility-specific seasonality, demand shocks, boarding, bed placement and capacity reconciliation are Phase 4 work. No bed or shift ledger is fabricated here.

## Clinical dependencies and detail

Six pathways select plausible diagnoses, selected procedures, analytes and observations. Opening known diseases influence subsequent pathway propensity. Patients under 40 are outside the selected COPD pathway; elective knee replacement starts at age 45 in this model. These age gates are scope choices, not universal medical rules. No pathway is chosen from a name or self-reported demographic category. Ambulatory/ED visits mix previously known chronic follow-up with episodic chest/upper-abdominal complaints and a small acute pneumonia component. They are not assigned one acute diagnosis for life. A chronic follow-up can use an opening assertion or an earlier observed admission, never a future diagnosis. Inpatient duration uses a variable central component plus a 5% long-stay mixture, with longer unplanned returns. A 3% medical nonresponse component can leave for further care with persistent/worsening measurements; such transferred pneumonia cases do not receive fabricated resolution or medical-readiness assertions. A terminal December cohort supplies observed adverse trajectories; other end-window admissions remain open.

The captured detail is deliberately bounded and non-exhaustive. Each inpatient pathway has two observation series, two analytes collected initially and again later, a procedure/education service, selected oral orders/administrations where configured, and assessment (including an operative note after each captured operation) plus closing documentation when observed. Acute scalar results share a latent illness state; recovery changes direction and known CKD changes creatinine context. Pneumonia inflammatory markers have a predeclared correlation check. PRN use declines over the selected recovery course. Minimum elapsed spacing, rolling 24-hour limits and Chicago local-day maxima are all enforced; a fall-back day cannot introduce an extra dose. Care outside this capture is unknown, rather than encoded as zero or asserted complete.

Each analyte, order, administration, dispense and document is one registered row. A repeated analyte has a standing analyte order and separate counted specimens/results. A laboratory result is clinically effective at collection and becomes known at result-ready time. Results may legitimately arrive after discharge; unfinished post-cutoff analysis stays unknown. Its immutable encounter reference preserves the collection-time snapshot. Related analytes share a specimen identity and collection time; they retain individual results and availability. Procedures use compatible earlier orders; medication conversions use the exact product's strength per base unit. Take-home dispensing is distinct from ingestion. Ward dispensing is not a second consumption event. A recorded matching allergy yields NOT_DONE, zero consumption and no stock group; it does not silently disappear.

The 30,000 immutable appointment rows contain 21,800 attended slots, 4,000 no-shows, 4,000 cancellations including 1,000 linked reschedules, and 200 future pending slots. Booking time is retained separately from the time the final extract knows an outcome. The single outcome row must never be treated as if every field were available at booking. Source booking/outcome replay will be implemented under the later source-delivery contract.

## Knowledge and longitudinal assertions

Encounters begin with arrival-only facts; inpatient admission and observed discharge are later knowledge revisions. An open row has no retrospectively filled discharge. Immutable patient, coverage and clinician version references resolve source knowledge when their assertion is created. Source knowledge and later file-export availability are separate clocks. Conditions have onset/status, typed problem/allergy/diagnosis meaning and distinct diagnosis rank. Acute pneumonia resolution appends a bitemporal closure and resolved assertion; earlier knowledge retains active disease. Newly recognized chronic problems become available for later histories. Missing conditions remain unknown.

Notes use the contemporaneous demographic name and age, working assessment, known problems, known measured values, explicitly administered/withheld/dispensed therapy and observed disposition. Six assessment openings and three uncertainty statements vary around factual content. Notes include source fact references for audit. An independent closed narrative grammar rejects changed values, future references, unsupported negatives, invented outcomes or arbitrary extra prose. Exact duplicate bodies are measured after removing patient identity, age and fact identifiers; raw IDs cannot satisfy the diversity threshold. Follow-up completion is never invented from advice.

The independent clinical death oracle uses encounter DEATH and any known registration death. The accepted registration extract has not yet received those terminal December notifications, so its null deceased_at remains unknown. No demographic row is overwritten or invented beyond the patient budget. Care after either observed death source is forbidden; source reconciliation of delayed registration is retained as an explicit later dependency.

## Budget and downstream boundary

The canonical release still has 25 registered business tables and 1,958,210 planned rows, below the two-million hard ceiling. This phase adds only encounter, appointment and clinical_event and extends clinical_condition; the candidate has 14 populated tables. Per-table envelopes remain enforced. The clinical-event family detail in the design is a planning allocation, not independent per-family caps. The implementation uses more explicit analyte/service orders and fewer captured administrations; actual family counts are published and all remain inside the same 580,000-event table allowance. No frozen schema, table envelope or overall record budget is changed. Allowances are not quotas: no meaningless result or duplicate lifecycle row is added to fill a planned count. Every candidate publishes actual counts, remaining allowance, checkpoint mirrors and displayed review mirrors. Temporary rebuild candidates each have their own manifest and are not part of a release's canonical count.

In-memory future plans guide generation but are never emitted as hidden fact tables or arrays. The persisted canonical clinical orders and encounter histories are the resource simulator's inputs. Their times and outcomes are provisional until Phases 4–5 integrate actual capacity and supplies; retiming must regenerate dependent observations, medication sequences, assertions, notes and validations together. No actual inventory or financial reconciliation is claimed at this gate.

Fifteen independent full-candidate rule groups execute schema, uniqueness, FK, master preservation, source availability, encounter and booking chronology, condition histories, terminology, service/order/specimen provenance, medications, contextual trajectories, narrative facts, longitudinal targets and row budgets. Deliberate invalid data, clean rebuild, cached resume, an actual process crash before SQLite commit, corrupt checkpoint rejection and a read-only persisted inspector exercise those controls end to end. Historical rule catalogs remain specified until their respective release checks are implemented; this scoped gate does not mark every final-release rule executed.
