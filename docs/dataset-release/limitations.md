# Modeled scope and acceptance limitations

Acceptance covers a coherent, configured fictional adult hospital group and the declared selected capture. It does not establish a complete real hospital population, clinical effectiveness, reimbursement compliance or vendor-certified integration.

- Patient and worker identities are fictional, drawn from pinned public aggregate name distributions and configured geography. No real EHR, individual contact or patient record is imported. Duplicate names are legitimate; stable keys distinguish people. Event-specific age uses local dates. People aged 100 at opening can naturally age to 103.
- Clinical pathways, observations, codes, orders, performed services and template-driven notes are checked for internal plausibility, provenance and knowledge timing. They are demonstration histories, not medical advice, epidemiologic benchmarks or validated treatment policies.
- The 30 selected late-window terminal outcomes exercise boundary/censoring behavior. This is not a mortality incidence or population survival series; mortality forecasting is outside the accepted scope.
- Version 1.1 revises six behaviours: ED stay length, medically-ready-to-discharge timing and the daytime discharge window, bed placement and cleaning response, weekday spreading of outpatient and elective visits, person-level appointment no-show propensity, claim-level denial propensity and seasonal RN absences. These are configured, internally consistent assumptions, not benchmarks of any real hospital. Appointment outcome totals (21,800 attended, 4,000 no-shows, 4,000 cancellations, 200 pending) stay at their frozen contract values; which bookings miss is probabilistic.
- RN overtime remains low (under 1% of RN hours). The fixed workforce holds about 88 RNs per hospital for roughly 14 RN shifts a day, so covering an absence seldom pushes a nurse past 40 weekly hours. Overtime forecasting is outside the accepted scope; absence and replacement patterns are in scope.
- Inpatient home discharges occur between 09:00 and 20:00 local time. Overnight inpatient departures appear only as transfers or deaths; ED visits end at any hour.
- Selected services, bundled local demonstration billing, sparse support/admin attendance and selected drug/supply use do not represent complete hospital billing, payroll or total cost of care. Prices are fictional assumptions, not validated reimbursement tariffs. Cost-to-care results apply to captured expense under the documented allocation policy.
- Prospective Medicare assignment models age eligibility at 65 and above. An under-65 disability Medicare cohort is not modeled. Original frozen payer targets remain configured fictional assumptions, not measured US prevalence.
- The security history is a selected grant/revocation cohort, not every currently authorized hospital user. The three experiment seeds share a fixed fictional catchment and public reference context; they test history sensitivity rather than independent demographic sampling.
- Axiom covers twelve illustrative healthcare financial planning archetypes. The approximately 900 customer-report inventory, tenant-specific layouts and vendor certification are not implemented. Workday workforce/hierarchy/security extracts and other feeds are local source simulations. Roster/time is a separate source, not a claim about a Workday tenant's modules.
- Version 1.1 is a new dataset baseline regenerated from the same master snapshot. Do not mix version 1.0.2 and 1.1.0 deliveries or incrementally merge one into the other; reload downstream consumers from version 1.1.0.
- The integrated master-context correction is a new dataset baseline. Do not incrementally merge older baseline deliveries under the same immutable version IDs. Rebuild downstream consumers for this release.
- Open stays, pending/unsubmitted claims, unbilled care, missing-not-yet-known values, missing plans, zero plans and unknown/censored outcomes remain distinct. Do not recode unknown follow-up as a negative outcome or interpret zero draft-claim AR as zero enterprise/unbilled AR. Readmission negatives require complete observed follow-up plus the validated source-delivery watermark.
- Joint-cell Wilson intervals are descriptive within month/facility/encounter class. Repeat visits are correlated; sparse-cell counts and domain constraints do not establish population-inference confidence intervals.
- Certification uses a fresh venv, hash-pinned dependencies and empty generated-data workspaces on the execution host with the same pinned Windows CPython base. It is not a separate-machine/container certification or a claim of byte-identical native formatting on every operating system. Canonical normalized business content is separately bound.
- Source snapshots, dictionaries, reports, indices, checkpoints, review summaries and comparison runs are technical mirrors/metadata with separate physical accounting. Only primary canonical business records and the bounded challenge/control copies are cap-counted release records; technical mirrors must never be appended as new business facts.
- Reviews are the user's delegated synthetic business, clinical, operations/workforce, finance and engineering review. There is no external licensed clinical, accountant or vendor endorsement. Final business-owner acceptance remains a separate explicit decision against the release descriptor and this document's hash.
- The package is a versioned local artifact with externally anchored integrity checks. Ordinary local files and registry records are not a security/WORM system. Production storage, operational SLAs, source integrations and analytics implementation are not delivered by this certification.

No limitation waives structural integrity, unexplained state/monetary differences, the record cap, essential entities, causal timing or source-contract failures. Any such defect blocks acceptance. Data acceptance permits a separately authorized solution workstream; it does not implement or deploy that workstream.
