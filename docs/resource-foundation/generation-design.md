The Chicago business window is January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2025; UTC instants span `[2023-01-01T06:00:00Z, 2026-01-01T06:00:00Z)`. Source delivery grace ends January 10, 2026.

The stage graph is `coordination -> workforce -> clinical_binding -> inventory -> validation`, with explicit dependencies on earlier stages. ## Workforce and historical knowledge

Nursing demand uses observed census at shift start, initial acuity weights and the fictional 5.5 productive RN hours per occupied patient-day assumption. At least one RN covers each ACUTE, ICU and ED scope. ACUTE is the shared medicine/surgery nursing pool. Critical-care nurses may cover ICU, ED or ACUTE; emergency nurses ED or ACUTE; medical-surgical nurses ACUTE or the selected circulating-OR role. The policy defines selected capture and does not establish clinical safe-staffing ratios.

Workers are eligible only within valid employment, credential and assignment periods. Assignment knowledge is separately resolved at each source revision. Staffing respects ten-hour minimum rest, thirteen-hour maximum elapsed shifts and sixty-hour maximum elapsed worked weeks. Nominal 07:00/19:00 local shifts retain eleven/thirteen elapsed hours on DST transitions; shorter flexible duties respond to demand. Surgeons work actual coordinated case lists, and one surgeon cannot perform simultaneous procedures. Concurrent operations require distinct actual circulating nurses and remain within two configured case slots per facility.

The 61,644 logical blocks have 66,582 retained assertions. Every modeled completed work block is retained. Selected earlier roster publications, all modeled absence/replacement histories and explicit late corrections preserve temporal behavior within the 70,000-version cap. Initial retained nursing rosters use lagged census available at publication, with fourteen-day lead; final future staffing is not copied into the plan. Changed demand creates explicit cancellation and changed planned/actual duration. Missing early history means unknown, never zero capacity.

There are 201 absence blocks and 198 agency replacements, with three employee replacements. February 2025 contains 104 absences, including thirty selected circulating-RN gaps. Absent originals have zero actual hours/cost; replacement workers have their own attended blocks and contract rates. Selected paid leave and sampled support/contractor activity are separately represented. The finite 300-worker master does not support a claim of complete payroll, continuous EVS staffing or a complete anesthetic team.

Elapsed worked hours equal productive + paid attended nonproductive + unpaid break hours. Paid hours exclude unpaid breaks. Overtime is the portion of paid hours above forty in the local Monday workweek, not an additional worked component. Week-crossing paid hours use reported six-place quantities, with the final micro-hour residual assigned to the final split. The fictional employee nonphysician cost adds a half loaded-rate premium for overtime; agency, contract and physician loaded rates are used once. This is a source-cost convention, not a statutory payroll calculation.

The service-start assignment rate is resolved using the attendance revision's knowledge time. Retroactive rates and 120 attendance corrections append versions. Latest/delivered views fold versions before summing expense. Nineteen closing in-progress blocks retain observed start, unknown actual end and zero finalized hours/cost. Later finance must explicitly handle any accrual rather than treating incomplete attendance as free care. Opening context and service dates do not invent an extra year of worked facts.

## Clinical constraints and authorship

Thirty operating-unit drape stockouts in September-November 2024 and thirty circulating-nurse absence windows in February 2025 postpone actual operative starts. General capacity queues also move services: 788 operations change, with a maximum delay of 180 minutes. Readiness is evaluated against the five-minute-before-planned-start case-cart requirement. Actual start/end, encounter revision context and source clocks change together. The original planned time and cause remain in counted clinical documents.

Delays fit inside the observed care intervals, so accepted discharge and bed times remain valid and unchanged. A delay beyond a care boundary or signed-document consistency boundary fails closed. All clinical and operational gates rerun. Staffing gaps never silently remove an occupied patient.

MED_ADMIN and selected observation authors are actual qualified working nurses in the right nursing scope. Operative authors are qualified surgeons with attendance covering the complete procedure. Other physician source authors represent ordering/review activity at occurrence, not continuous personal performance of every lab, therapy or dispense. Specialty-qualified attending-of-record and planned appointment providers retain their original meanings; they can differ from actual source authors. Operative documents are reviewing-physician source notes, not a claim of a complete dictated operative report.

Each resource-response note cites two exact previously recorded patient assessments, with value, unit, logical record and observed time. The independent validator checks their existence and availability and prohibits unsupported future outcomes. These are factual synthetic operational notes; they are not clinical recommendations.

## Stock and resource quantities

The catalog retains its existing scalar base/pack conversions. Explicit fictional supplier prices, calendar escalator and prospective planning quantities live in configuration. New receipt prices create new lots; transfers, returns and reversals preserve original lot valuation. `expires_on` is the exclusive local date: stock is removed at the start of that date under this synthetic source contract. Orders use on-hand/in-flight stock, fourteen-day reorder cover, thirty-day target cover and two-day procurement lead. Stock expiring within that lead does not falsely count as usable replenishment cover. Receipts use whole catalog packs; opening stock can include opened packs.

Facility PHARM is the controlled medication stock pool; it does not claim separate stock in every ward cabinet. Selected supplies reside in DIAG, SUPPORT and OR. Each administered dose or take-home dispense links to its lot/day/item/location consumption group. Cancelled/not-done administrations consume zero. MED_DISPENSE is take-home supply, not a second issue of already administered ward medication.

Single medication lines are indivisible administrations or whole prescription fills. Eligible FEFO considers lots sufficient for the complete line; residual stock is retained for smaller later fills. Supply requirements may span lots, using separately counted groups. Event-time replay independently verifies availability at each represented use, not merely a nonnegative day-end balance.

Selected supplies are one serum tube, one preparation pad and two gloves per distinct modeled specimen, one drape and two gloves per actual selected operation, and one gown per recorded precaution assessment. Multiple analytes on the same specimen do not multiply collection supplies. This is not a complete bill of materials: implants, anesthetics, blood products and unmodeled materials have no invented resource cost. Product/therapy substitutions are not asserted; the selected shortages resolve through transfers, delay or agency response, preserving original clinical intent.

Source CONSUME rows aggregate same-day use by lot/item/location. Their daily batch recording/delivery can lag care. Separate nonpatient waste consumes 78 base units, USD 11.41. Thirty unopened drape returns use paired OR transfer-out/SUPPORT return movements. Thirty aged opening cup lots plus ordinary residual lots produce 39 expiry movements. The closing count batch contains thirty distinct source variance lines, each retaining an initial loss of two, linked reversal of two and corrected loss of one. Lines are additive quantity adjustments and can share a stock-pool/lot; they are not successive full-pool recount quantities. This bounded correction scenario has a net USD 2.70 loss, not a reconciliation plug.

The 878 lots and 47,839 movements reconcile patient consumption USD 118,574.25, nonpatient consumption USD 11.41 and closing inventory USD 6,210.92. Receipt purchases, consumption, expiry, adjustments and closing assets remain distinct inputs for later accounting. Source labor totals USD 47,658,801.10 across the captured history. Neither stock-group costs nor whole-shift costs are automatically patient-level charges or allocated episode costs.
