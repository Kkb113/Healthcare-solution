# Operational generation design

The fictional hospitals retain their actual accepted identities, named patients, source demographic histories, coverage and credential snapshots. Calendar sampling regenerates coherent source encounters and clinical evidence together; it does not independently randomize joined fact tables. A SHA-256 anchor verifies every file in the accepted clinical parent, and the independent master validator rechecks the immutable master input.

## Demand and clinical dependencies

Seeded integer-weight calendar sampling combines distinct site seasonal phases, modest annual growth, weekday effects, daily variation and a finite January 8-February 25, 2024 respiratory surge. Respiratory admission dates are conditioned on that surge; ambulatory respiratory presentations also increase. Arrival-hour distributions vary across the day; elective proposals retain weekday morning bookings. All 36 monthly ED arrival rates use local calendar days, including leap-year February. Realized daily respiratory/weather indices become available six hours after local midnight closing the observed day. Calendar publication is separately timestamped. The partial holiday calendar explicitly includes New Year, July 4 and Christmas; it is not represented as a complete federal holiday source.

The original boundary cohort is spread across December rather than forcing all end-open/death test cases into the same two days. An outcome is observed only if its actual completion precedes the cutoff. Names and ages remain sourced from event-time accepted patient versions, including the February 29 age convention.

An explicit selected placement assessment records a suspected transmissible respiratory presentation and a private isolation-capable-room request. Its source timestamp precedes placement. The simulator consumes this source assertion; pneumonia diagnosis or acuity alone does not imply that every case needs isolation. Shared ordinary rooms require matching recorded M/F administrative sex. X or UNKNOWN registrations default to a private room; matching unknown values do not establish compatibility. An isolation patient occupies the room alone. ICU rooms are already single-bed. These are fictional operational assumptions, not clinical protocols.

## Flow and independent bed dimensions

Administrative inpatient admission starts care. Initial ED triage is captured by a selected oxygen-saturation observation five minutes after ED arrival. Admission decision, admission and physical bed placement are separate. A patient can receive selected care in a monitored boarding area while a compatible bed is prepared. Boarding is excluded from licensed-bed occupancy. Elective inpatient arrival occurs when a weekday daytime bed offer is available; the original booking remains visible as pre-admission deferral rather than being counted as weeks of inpatient care. Delayed placement delays completion of the modeled ward stabilization work; histories, acute resolution, follow-ups, source snapshots, orders, measurements, administrations and grounded notes are rebuilt before publication. Actual staffing and inventory may cause a further integrated rerun in Phase 5.

MEDICINE and SURGERY are adult med-surg flex wards for the selected medical and postoperative cohorts, with preferred responsibility and reciprocal overflow. A configured 20 percent of acuity 2 medical admissions, and acuity 1 medical admissions, receive short ICU stabilization; after two days, stepdown requests a compatible ward bed while retaining the ICU bed until relocation. A stable ward patient may temporarily use an otherwise idle ICU location, with a soft buffer of one remaining available ICU bed per site. Such placement is explicitly marked WARD_OVERFLOW_ICU and requests ward relocation after twelve hours. Physical ICU location does not assert intensive care treatment or justify an ICU charge.

Each bed event records its complete post-state: occupancy/cleanliness, service state, staffing enablement, reservation and immutable source context. Licensed inventory does not change when beds close. An occupied patient is never erased to make a staffing total balance. Maintenance requests wait until the bed is empty and clean. Twelve staged summer 2024 closures last 962 bed-days in total; actual start may follow the requested date. Vacating creates DIRTY, environmental-services work creates CLEANING, and observed completion creates READY. Turnaround varies from 60 to 90 minutes. Reservations are nonoccupancy holds. Cancelled holds produce RELEASE and no patient occupancy.

57 opening occupied beds are represented by actual patient encounters, balanced across three sites and checked for compatibility. Critical acuity 1 remains first. Elective requests aged seven days gain priority during weekday daytime windows to prevent starvation; other requests use initial acuity, request time and stable identity. Acute requests still without a compatible bed after 24 hours have an observed external TRANSFER disposition and a separate source note with the capacity cause. No receiving care or later death is invented outside the network. An observed death before that escalation can remain a documented monitored-boarding death without a licensed-bed interval. The simulator records capacity/precaution/maintenance causes separately from actual placement timestamps. Queues remain explicit through admission milestones and absence of placement; no arrival is silently deleted.

## Transfers, timing and reconciliation

Ward transfers retain encounter identity. Source VACATE and destination OCCUPY share a correlation and one effective instant. Twelve continuous cross-hospital episodes receive a separate destination encounter; they add twelve physical care contexts, not twelve new network admissions or readmissions. Sending care must actually finish before the route-dependent transport and handover gap and receiving arrival. Receiving boarding remains distinct from transport. Other TRANSFER dispositions leave the modeled network; no external receiving facts are invented.

Linked readmission encounters cannot start during an unfinished prior admission. Their linked ED visits move together. Conflicting free ambulatory/ED visits are moved to an available care slot. Original appointment slots remain in scheduled_start/scheduled_end, actual arrival is recorded separately, and outcomes are unavailable before observation. No synthetic pre-cutoff discharge or resolution is retained when care remains open at cutoff.

All bed intervals and current-state representations are computed views. No additional business table, opaque fact array or hidden occupancy cohort is published. The event-state occupied-seconds integral must equal both independently paired bed intervals and the simulator's separately accumulated interval digest. Census deltas reconcile at every occupancy boundary and by facility. Boarding plus occupied care plus explicit gaps reconciles to admitted care time; cross-site transport is outside the two facility encounters.

## Corrections and publication

One hundred source READY assertions have an erroneous service-state entry and an immutable later CORRECTION at the same effective instant. Replay preserves the earlier delivered assertion for historical knowledge queries, then replaces it once the correction dependency is available. A correction delivered before its original waits for the original. Reversed delivery and duplicate retransmission converge. Conflicting duplicate IDs and incomplete final correction chains fail.

The profile uses transactional SQLite checkpoints, a single-writer lock, explicit dependencies, deterministic metadata, checksummed canonical JSONL and atomic readiness markers. Data/source-code/config/runtime changes invalidate affected stages before recomputation. Review-only changes invalidate validation while retaining generated facts. A real process-exit test proves uncommitted validation rollback. Physical checkpoint and review mirrors are counted separately from canonical business rows. All canonical versions, corrections, carry-ins and selected scalar clinical facts remain inside the frozen table envelopes; unused allowances are not padded.

The operational scope explicitly permits actual elective arrival from 07:00 through 14:59 local on weekdays, while original booking proposals remain morning appointments. This uses afternoon-ready capacity and keeps the selected procedure within the daytime/early-evening care window. The standalone provisional clinical validator retains its original 07:00-10:59 default; an explicit operational policy addendum is required for the wider window.

Review rendering orders every placement interval by actual start time, including ICU-first stays, and uses that first interval for waiting-time labels. Source assertions sharing an effective instant are displayed in per-bed source sequence. Review or validator code changes invalidate validation rather than perturbing deterministic generated business metadata.

Boarding starts at the admission decision. Administrative admission-to-placement wait is a separately reconciled component; the external-transfer escalation clock is 24 hours from administrative inpatient admission. Report first-placement/disposition latency only for observed outcomes, and report still-open unplaced requests as right-censored lower bounds. Cross-site transport seconds are reported separately from admitted care and occupied-bed time.

Sending network referrals have prospective coordination documents known at admission, with actual handoff time explicitly unobserved. Receiving coordination receipts confirm the observed arrival without asserting a later outcome. Route-specific synthetic ground-transport assumptions are 120 minutes Peoria-Springfield, 180 minutes Peoria-Rockford and 240 minutes Rockford-Springfield, plus 0-30 minutes of handover variation. They are modeling assumptions, not ambulance travel quotations. Administrative capacity transfers and continuity transfers do not force every clinical measurement to worsen; the seeded illness-response component remains distinct from transfer cause.
