# Explaining financial differences

Phase 6 creates causal differences between plans and actuals using the accepted activity histories. A planning amount is the independently reproducible model estimate at its publication, including its uncertainty term. It is not a percentage perturbation of the eventual actual. This guide identifies the counted facts needed to explain a difference; it does not implement an analytics product.

Select the same facility, center, account, target month, currency and planning vintage before comparing. Select one actual vintage and one budget vintage; do not add replacements. Budget statistics at facility administration are not directly comparable to a single unit's actual statistics. Accounts or units without a comparable plan remain missing. A zero budget has no percentage variance.

| Driver | Actual facts and financial consequence | Planning reference and reconciliation |
|---|---|---|
| Demand and capacity | Actual occupied bed intervals produce room days; ED/ambulatory attendance produces facility services; selected completed care produces ancillary lines. Boarding produces separate extended holding services after the bundled allowance. Capacity/stock/staff delays affect actual timing and service mix. | Published capacity, occupancy, ED/ambulatory demand and length-of-stay assumptions feed nominal revenue. Known completed posting months feed later estimates. Charge units × stored prices reproduce billed gross; source recognition groups reconcile to monthly GROSS_REVENUE. |
| Service and payer mix | ROOM/ICU/holding/attendance/operative/pharmacy/lab charges retain service categories and exact encounter coverage. Payer `allowed_factor` and line review changes alter prospective and subsequent net revenue. | Published price/allowed assumptions are distinct from eventual realized mix. Gross and contra-revenue must be compared separately; cash is not net revenue. Charge-to-line-to-event source predicates reproduce every resulting journal group. |
| Price and loaded-rate changes | Charge prices have a declared annual escalator. Delivered worker assignment rates, attendance revisions and contract/agency worker types determine captured labor costs. | The nominal model uses published assumptions and delivered workforce assertions. Its historical component uses only source-known postings. Future retroactive corrections cannot rewrite an earlier plan. |
| Agency replacement, overtime and absence | Accepted shift versions distinguish absent originals, agency replacements, actual paid/productive hours, overtime and loaded costs. An absent zero-cost block is not costed again alongside its replacement. ADMIN salary top-ups subtract captured shift expense. | Compare known staffing/rate assumptions with final selected attendance, then retain separate late rate/attendance true-ups. Signed source deltas telescope to accepted shift cost; monthly pool reclassification has zero net expense. |
| Denial review and recovery | Initial denials retain disputed receivables. Appeals retain the original claim identity. Contractual review changes affect contra revenue; explicit final credit losses affect OTHER_EXPENSE; payments affect CASH/AR. | Earlier plans cannot know a later appeal outcome. Claim-event categories explain the resulting accounting differences without treating every denial as lost revenue or counting resubmissions as new originals. |
| Resource use and waste | Actual lot consumption produces drug/supply expense; purchases remain inventory/AP until consumption. Waste, expiry and count corrections are explicit nonpatient OTHER_EXPENSE. | Budget cost assumptions differ from realized selected resource use. Inventory opening + receipts − issues/expiry/count changes equals closing asset; only consumption and explicit losses enter expense. |
| Period and publication timing | Every source journal preserves service dates, posting date, knowledge time and a reconstructible source-group predicate. December has an initial and final actual close. | Compare the same target period and as-of. Separate genuine service/rate differences from late open-period postings and replacement actuals. Do not force a service-month control to equal a posting-month actual without the explicit bridge. |

For example, reviewed trace 32 has 14 billable holding hours, two ICU room days, three acute room days split across June/July, and a completed respiratory assessment. Its USD 14,370.10 original billed amount is reproduced from those stored quantities/prices; contractual estimates and later settlements remain separate. Traces 10–12 show final disputed-line losses; traces 13–15 show pending disputes without automatic loss; traces 22–24 show cost-bearing unplaced holding with an explicit holding service. The [bound sample](../../reviews/financial-history-sample.md) includes their exact dates, sources, journal groups and allocation runs.

A single grouped journal can cover several patients or shifts. Reconstruct its declared source predicate before explaining its contribution; do not assign its whole amount to each linked patient. Selected patient/nonpatient allocations exhaust each eligible pool once and create no new expense. The technical gate independently checks source-to-journal and journal-to-Axiom equality. Driver attribution beyond these captured facts must remain an explicitly labeled analysis, rather than a new uncounted business fact or an unexplained balancing residual.
