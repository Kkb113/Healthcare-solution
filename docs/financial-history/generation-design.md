## Facility charges and reimbursement

Five appended HOSPITAL_LOCAL PROCEDURE vocabulary rows represent actual source billing categories: acute room day, ICU room day, ED facility attendance, ambulatory facility attendance and extended monitored holding hours. They are not invented CPT/UB-04 codes. Prices and annual escalators are fictional published configuration inputs. Room days start at actual physical bed placement and exclude the final physical discharge day, minimum one for same-day occupancy. Clinical admission and placement can differ. Boarding and entirely unplaced admissions do not create fictitious room charges. Responsibility follows the first occupied bed on the initial partial day and the bed at midnight thereafter; a transfer across midnight uses the first actual occupied bed that day. Charges split by month, center and ICU/acute category and await contributing occupancy knowledge. Carry-in occupants do not receive an extra discharge-day minimum.

One separately captured completed ancillary line per encounter prefers actual operative care; otherwise a keyed mix selects an actual lab result, medication issue or performed procedure. The line references that exact event/catalog version and actual quantity. Other routine care is bundled/outside selected itemization; these are not exhaustive daily dose or procedure claims. Charge units, stored prices, service dates and billed amounts independently reconcile.

Accepted coverage and payer `allowed_factor` provide a prospective synthetic estimate. CASE_RATE/FEE_SCHEDULE descriptors do not implement real Medicare DRG, negotiated payer schedules or Medicaid tariffs. Authorization status and knowledge are explicit, separate from later denial outcome. Claim lines preserve the original charge and prospective estimate. One original institutional receivable account spans its selected lines, with submission/appeal attempts as events. SELF_PAY statements are separately identifiable, as described in the budget amendment.

The payer authorization flag applies only to configured elective room, operative and advanced diagnostic services. Emergency/routine attendance, ordinary labs and pharmacy do not receive blanket prior-authorization requirements. Declined/expired authorization causes initial denial of the affected line, pending status increases review probability, and reason codes distinguish expiry from missing authorization. Subsequent appeal/review can resolve coverage explicitly.

Extended monitored holding before first physical placement, or an entirely unplaced admission, has an explicit hourly facility service. The first four elapsed holding hours are bundled; only excess observed hours are billed as OTHER / holding_hours at the declared local tariff. It does not assert a licensed bed. Month splits conserve stored six-place total hours with the final micro-hour residual in the last segment. Placement/closed-care knowledge governs availability, and open holding stops at the observed cutoff. This closes the review finding where long holding had cost but only an ancillary charge.

## Recognition, responsibility and AR

Completed eligible service periods recognize Dr AR / Cr gross revenue and Dr contra revenue / Cr AR for the contractual estimate. Service AR stays unbilled until submission/statement. SUBMITTED assigns the prospective net amount into billed claim AR; it adds zero new revenue, zero cash and zero net GL AR. Unclaimed/open/draft services remain identifiable unbilled balances.

Initial payer review can change accepted-line estimates by a small configured synthetic review variation. A line-level denial does not itself write off AR. Partial approvals allocate accepted responsibility, leaving the denied line disputed. Appeals/resubmissions preserve original identity; recoveries assign previously unassigned responsibility, while explicit final loss extinguishes the denied balance. Payer/patient liability is assigned responsibility, not cash or remaining AR. Patient-statement credit losses are distinct from disputed payer losses.

Payer receipts may be partial, with a later remainder settlement. Patient collections vary with obligation size and include full/partial payments, unresolved balances and selected final credit losses after 180 days. Forgiveness reduces assigned patient responsibility and AR explicitly. A refund or reversal references a prior immutable receipt, reverses its exact signed cash/AR effect and may have a later replacement payment. No economic claim event occurs at/after `2026-01-01T06:00:00Z`, even if delivery is later. Immutable claim headers with an observed first-submission field are exported only when that submission is source-known. Earlier uncaptured drafts are unknown.

Opening insured claims preserve original billed/contractual/cash/writeoff components with positive opening AR. Their header/line snapshot is first published at the window opening, without exposing future carry-in membership to earlier queries. Their OPENING event has zero current AR/cash/revenue movement. Opening ledger journals establish the asset once. Historical charge intervals create no current revenue/admission/census, even when their source encounter snapshot predates discharge. Enterprise AR equals billed original-account AR plus current unbilled prospective service AR, including explicit current SELF_PAY/open-visit balances.

## Allocation

Labor drivers are captured patient hours at responsible units, ACUTE medical/surgical coverage and actual operative case minutes. Unsupported support/admin labor is explicit NONPATIENT.
